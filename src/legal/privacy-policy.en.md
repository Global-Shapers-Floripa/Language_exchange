## Clause 2 - Legal Definitions

For the purposes of this Policy, the definitions set out in Article 5 of the LGPD apply, notably:

- Personal Data: information related to an identified or identifiable natural person;
- Sensitive Personal Data: data concerning racial or ethnic origin, religious belief, political opinion, membership in a union or in a religious, philosophical, or political organization, data concerning health or sexual life, genetic or biometric data;
- Anonymized Data: data relating to a data subject who cannot be identified, taking into account the use of reasonable and available technical means at the time of processing;
- Processing: any operation carried out with personal data, such as collection, production, receipt, classification, use, access, reproduction, transmission, distribution, processing, filing, storage, elimination, evaluation or control of the information, modification, communication, transfer, dissemination, or extraction;
- Data Subject: the natural person to whom the personal data being processed relates;
- Controller: a natural or legal person, under public or private law, responsible for decisions regarding the processing of data;
- Processor: a natural or legal person, under public or private law, that processes data on behalf of the controller;
- Consent: a free, informed, and unambiguous expression by which the data subject agrees to the processing of their personal data for a specified purpose;

# CHAPTER II - PERSONAL DATA COLLECTED

## Clause 3 - Categories of Data Collected

The Florianópolis Hub collects the following categories of personal data:

a) Data for Identification and Registration purposes:

- Full name;
- Email address;
- Country and city of residence;
- Profile photo;
- Date of birth (for age-eligibility verification);
- Profile information related to the Global Shapers community (the Hub the User participates in);

b) Data for Language-Learning and Educational purposes:

- Languages spoken (native or fluent) and respective proficiency levels;
- Languages the User wishes to learn;
- Schedule availability;
- History of sessions held (dates, duration, Language Partner).

## Clause 4 - Data Not Collected

The Language Exchange Project platform does not have as its purpose the collection of sensitive personal data (as defined in Article 5, II, of the LGPD), such as health data, sexual orientation, racial or ethnic origin, or religious or political belief. Should the User voluntarily share such information in their communications, the Florianópolis Hub will apply appropriate processing and protection measures to such data.

## Clause 6 - Purposes and Legal Bases

The Florianópolis Hub processes personal data for the following purposes, with the corresponding legal bases set out in Article 7 of the LGPD:

a) Execution of procedures related to the use of the platform (Art. 7, V, LGPD):

- Creation and management of the User's Account;
- Matching between compatible Language Partners;
- Operation of the platform;
- Organization of community activities;
- Scheduling and management of learning sessions;
- Communication with the User regarding the operation of the Platform.

b) Compliance with Legal or Regulatory Obligations (Art. 7, II, LGPD):

- Responding to judicial or administrative orders or requests from competent authorities.

c) Based on the Data Subject's Consent (Art. 7, I, LGPD):

- Preparation of anonymized statistics on Platform usage (geographic distribution of users, most common language pairings, session frequency, etc.), for the purposes of improvement and publication of impact reports;
- Sending informational communications about news from the Platform and the Global Shapers network;
- Use of profile photo and language data for visibility in the Platform's internal directory.

d) Legitimate Interest of the Controller (Art. 7, IX, LGPD):

- Prevention of fraud, abuse, and violations of the Terms of Use;
- Security and integrity of the Platform;
- Continuous improvement of matching algorithms;
- Improvement of the User experience.

# CHAPTER V - DATA SHARING

## Clause 7 - Sharing with Third Parties

The Florianópolis Hub may share users' personal data in the following circumstances:

a) With other users (for the purpose of forming language pairs):

When forming a Language Partnership, the following data will be visible to the partner: name, profile photo (if provided), country/city of residence, languages spoken, and learning goals. The User is aware of, and consents to, this sharing upon accepting the Platform's Terms of Use.

b) With Service Providers (Processors):

The Florianópolis Hub may engage service providers for technological operations (security, analytics). These providers will act as Processors, bound by the Hub's instructions and required to observe equivalent levels of data protection.

c) With the Global Shapers Network / World Economic Forum:

Statistical and anonymized data on the Platform's impact may be shared with the Global Shapers network and/or the World Economic Forum, exclusively for the purposes of social impact reporting. Under no circumstances will data allowing the individual identification of users be shared without their authorization.

d) By Legal or Judicial Determination:

The Florianópolis Hub may disclose personal data in compliance with a legal obligation, judicial order, or determination by a competent authority, to the extent strictly necessary, as established by Art. 7, II, of the LGPD, and in light of the principles set out in Art. 6 of the same law.

## Clause 8 - International Data Transfer

Due to the global nature of the Platform, users' personal data may be transferred to, or processed in, countries other than the User's country of residence, subject to the safeguards provided for in this Policy and in the LGPD, and to Users' specific consent where required.

The Platform will adopt appropriate technical and administrative measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, leaks, loss, improper alteration, and/or destruction.

In the event of a security incident that may pose a relevant risk or harm to users, the Florianópolis Hub will notify the ANPD (Brazilian National Data Protection Authority) and affected Data Subjects within a maximum of 72 (seventy-two) hours of confirming the incident, in accordance with Art. 48 of the LGPD, including:

i. a description of the nature of the personal data affected;
ii. information about the affected users;
iii. an indication of the technical and security measures adopted to protect the data;
iv. the risks related to the incident and the measures adopted to reverse or mitigate its effects;
v. the reasons for any delay, in cases where the notification was not immediate.
